Instruments

Guitar

Acoustic and electric, chords to songs you actually want to play.

Violin

Bow control, ear training and stage confidence, at a pace that suits the student.

Sitar

Classical technique on an instrument with its own voice and its own discipline.

Tabla

Hand technique and rhythm cycles. Beginners welcome, ages 4 to adult.

Singing & Choir

Breathing, pitch and repertoire — solo work, plus choir for those who want it.

Recorder

The gentlest first instrument — often the right start for the youngest students.

How lessons work

Three steps, no long commitment.

  1. Book a trial class on WhatsApp

    Message 0315-0252001 with the instrument and the student's age. You will get a time for a first class.

  2. Meet the instructor, one-on-one

    The first class is a proper lesson, not a sales meeting: technique, a piece to take home, and an honest read on where to start.

  3. Keep going, then perform

    Students who are ready take the stage in our recital series — the last one was at NAPA, Karachi.

Performance

A stage is part of the curriculum.

The Grand Recital '26 brought students to NAPA, Karachi — solo piano and violin pieces in front of a full room. The series continues; more performances are announced on @themusic_school.

Every student who wants to perform gets prepared for it in class, not on the day.
